# INBOX

> Temporary staging area for durable information awaiting routing to a project or reference file.

## Rules

- Keep entries short.
- Add a date when the information matters over time.
- Move durable information into the relevant project file when its destination becomes clear.
- Remove staged entries once their information has been routed; archive superseded entries only when useful.
- Do not use this as a conversation log.
- Do not store passwords, access tokens, payment-card data, government IDs, or other secrets here.
